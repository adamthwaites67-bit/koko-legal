Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 September 2026

Everything you track stays on your phone. We cannot see your cycles, your symptoms or your notes, and neither can anyone else.

Koko is a period and cycle tracking app. For UK data protection law we are the data controller for the limited personal data described below. Contact us about privacy at help.koko@outlook.com.

Where things stand today

As of this version:

  • Everything you track is stored only on your phone. There is no cloud copy of your cycles, symptoms or notes.
  • You sign in with an email address or with Apple. That identifier is the only personal detail we hold, and it is stored in London.
  • There is no analytics, advertising or tracking of any kind in the app.
  • The only things that ever leave your phone are the identifier you sign in with and feedback you choose to send us.
  • Sharing your cycle with a partner or friend is not available yet.

What the app stores on your device

The app stores what you choose to record, in a database on your phone. Depending on what you use, that can include:

  • Cycle information: period start and end dates, flow, spotting, discharge, predicted dates and fertile windows.
  • Body signals: basal body temperature and ovulation test results, where you log them.
  • How you feel: symptoms, pain severity, mood, energy, sleep, and any notes you write.
  • Everyday context: menstrual products, food and supplements, relief activities, and how symptoms affected your day.
  • Your profile: the name you enter, your date of birth, and your region.
  • Your settings and progress: reminders, preferences, streaks, badges and your companion.

This is special category data under UK GDPR, which deserves the highest level of protection. Our approach is not to collect it: it is written to your device and stays there.

We have no ability to access, read, recover or restore this information. If you lose your phone or delete the app, it is gone. That is a deliberate trade in favour of your privacy, and you should know about it.

Feedback you send us

If you use Send feedback in Profile, we receive:

  • how you said you are finding the app;
  • whatever you type in the message box;
  • your app version, device model and operating system version, so we can reproduce problems;
  • your email address, only if you choose to enter one so we can reply.

Nothing about your cycle, your symptoms or your predictions is attached. Only what you typed.

This is delivered and stored through Google Forms, acting as our processor, and may be processed outside the UK under Google’s standard safeguards.

Please do not put health information in the message box. We do not need it, and it would place information in our records that would otherwise never leave your phone.

Our lawful basis is your consent, given by choosing to send the message. We keep feedback for up to 24 months, then delete it.

Your camera

One relief exercise can show a live view from your front camera, so you can see your own posture while you move, like a mirror.

Nothing is recorded, captured, photographed, saved or sent. The image exists only on screen while that exercise is open, and the app contains no code capable of taking a photo or a video.

You can decline camera access and the exercise still works, and you can withdraw it at any time in iOS Settings.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app can remind you to log, or tell you a period may be due. These are local notifications scheduled by your own device. No server is involved and no notification content reaches us.

Your account

Creating an account stores one thing about you: the identifier you signed in with. It is held by Supabase, who run our authentication on our behalf, on servers in London. Nothing else about you is sent when you sign in.

You can sign in with an email address or with Apple. If you use Apple, we receive whatever Apple chooses to give us, and you may tell Apple to hide your real address, in which case we only ever see a relay address and never learn your own. We are not told anything else about your Apple account.

We use that identifier only to sign you in and, if you ever ask us something, to reply to you. It is never used for marketing and never shared. The lawful basis is performance of a contract: you cannot have an account without an identifier for it.

An account does not move your health data off your phone. Your cycles, symptoms and notes stay on this device exactly as before.

Deleting your account deletes the identifier we hold. Because your health data was never uploaded, deleting the app removes all of that at the same time.

If you turn on backup, sync or sharing

None of these are available yet. If we add them, each would be something you switch on, never something enabled on your behalf, and you would be told exactly what would leave your device before you turned it on.

  • Backup or sync would store an encrypted copy of your entries with our hosting provider so you could restore them or use more than one device.
  • Sharing with a partner or friend would send only the specific things you chose to share with the specific person you chose, and you would be able to change or withdraw it at any time.

Until then, none of this happens and there is nothing to opt out of.

Analytics

The app contains no analytics, advertising, attribution or crash reporting service, and no third-party tracker of any kind. We do not profile you and we do not sell or share your data.

If we ever add analytics to understand which features are used, it would measure app usage only, never the contents of what you log, and this policy would say so before it shipped.

Subscriptions and payment

Koko offers an optional paid subscription. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through your App Store account.

We never see or receive your card details, billing address or payment information. Apple tells us only whether an active subscription exists.

Children

Koko is not intended for children under 13. We ask for your date of birth during setup to check this. If you are under the age at which you can consent for yourself where you live, you should have a parent or guardian’s agreement before using the app.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to and port your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Those rights apply to data we hold, which today is only the feedback described above. Email help.koko@outlook.com and we will respond within one month.

For everything the app stores on your phone you already have complete and immediate control without asking us: it is all visible in the app, you can edit or delete any entry, and deleting the app removes all of it. We cannot action a request about that data on your behalf, because we cannot reach it.

Security

Your data is protected by your device’s own security: your passcode, Face ID or Touch ID, and the iOS sandbox, which stops other apps reading our database. We strongly recommend keeping a passcode set, because an unlocked phone gives anyone holding it the same access you have.

Because your health data is never transmitted, it cannot be intercepted in transit or exposed in a breach of our systems.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, where the change is significant, tell you in the app before it takes effect.

Any future change that would cause health information to leave your device will be introduced as something you are asked to turn on, not something enabled on your behalf.

Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, please tell us first at help.koko@outlook.com.
